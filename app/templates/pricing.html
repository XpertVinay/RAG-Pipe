Frequently Asked Questions

How does Admin User Invitation work?

Standard self-registration creates accounts with common user privileges. As an administrator, you can open the Team Invites dashboard and generate secure, time-limited invite links specifying elevated roles (such as Staff or Admin) and optionally bound to a specific colleague's email address.

Can I upgrade or switch billing cycles anytime?

Yes! When you upgrade, your organization's maximum seats and privileges are unlocked immediately. Annual billing provides an automatic 20% discount.

Is payment processing secure?

All transactions use encrypted SSL connections. In demo environments, payments are safely processed through our simulated checkout pipeline without charging your real credit card.

How is document privacy protected?

Workspaces are isolated with strict RBAC permission checks. Private rooms are only accessible to authorized members configured by the room owner or organization admin.
