🛡️ Security & Defense
Securing Vector Stores & Prompt Injections

OWASP Top 10 for LLMs identifies indirect prompt injection, vector data poisoning, and unauthorized retrieval leakage as paramount threats to enterprise generative AI.

🔒 How to Become Safe & Secure
  • 1. Passkeys & MFA: Mandate hardware FIDO2 or authenticator apps; eliminate SMS OTPs to block credential phishing.
  • 2. Zero-Trust & RBAC: Segment workspace rooms so sensitive documents are never indexed into general pools.
  • 3. Encryption at Rest & Transit: Enforce AES-256 for vector embeddings and TLS 1.3 for all streaming SSE channels.
  • 4. Prompt Injection Guardrails: Sanitize all retrieval inputs and scrub PII or secrets before LLM synthesis.
  • 5. Immutable Backups & Audit Logs: Maintain tamper-proof revision trails and rotate API keys regularly.
